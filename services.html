Before you ask FAQ

Questions, answered

What does a custom project cost?

Starting prices are listed in starting prices above. Beyond that every build is quoted on scope — how many models, how much pixel art, and whether there’s plugin work tying it together. Tell us what you’re planning in Discord and you’ll get a price and a timeline before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the brief. A single model or an icon set moves quickly; a full interface pass or a plugin with a custom system takes longer. You get a timeline with the quote, and you’ll see work-in-progress as we go rather than one drop at the end.

Can you match my server’s existing style?

Yes — that’s most of what we do. Send references, your current resource pack, or screenshots of the server, and new assets get built to sit next to what you already have instead of fighting it.

How fast do you reply?

Within 24 hours on our Discord, usually much sooner. Open a ticket with what you need and you’ll get a scope question or a quote back on the same thread.
