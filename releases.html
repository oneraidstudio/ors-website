Questions, answered

What do I get in All Your Iconz?

150 pixel-art server icons for Minecraft, delivered as ready-to-use image files. Swap them through the year — seasonal events, updates, themed weekends — or use them across your store pages and Discord.

Are the emoji packs actually free?

Yes. All Your Meme Emojis (45 emojis) and All Your Hamster Emojis (25 emojis) are completely free on MCModels — download them, drop them into your chat setup, and you’re done.

Can you customize a pack for my server?

Yes. If a pack is close but not quite your style, hop into our Discord — recolors and small tweaks are quick, and bigger changes or fully custom packs fall under our services.
